Why I chose DevOps

I am fascinated by the entire journey of software — from the moment a developer writes code, to the instant users interact with it in production. That journey, and everything required to make it fast, reliable, automated, and secure, is exactly what DevOps engineering is about.

I chose DevOps and Cloud Engineering because I want to work at the intersection of infrastructure, automation, and reliability. Building CI/CD pipelines, provisioning cloud infrastructure with Terraform, orchestrating containers on Kubernetes, and integrating security into every pipeline stage — these are not just academic exercises. I have built, broken, debugged, and fixed real infrastructure across 8+ projects.
